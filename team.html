The team that makes it possible.

The-Anh Dao

Founder and Director

Concordia International School Hanoi

Hanoi, Vietnam

The Anh created SubstitOud to combat the health and sustainability problems of incense's 6-millennia tradition. Currently, he focuses on the cultural integration of SubstitOud across Vietnam. Outside, he is passionate about religious philosophy and olfaction.

Karan Verma

Material Researcher & Growth Lead

Liberty High School

Texas, USA

Karan refines the smokeless incense to reduce harmful emissions by prioritizing sustainable bio-based materials. He also leads temple expansion efforts and is the co-founder of Naturista Coffee Pods.

Tim Kasian

Research Lead

The Taft School

Dnipro, Ukraine

Tim oversees all of SubstitOud's research on cultural perception that guides much of the project's philosophy. Outside, Tim is an avid combinatorial number theory researcher specializing in integer mosaics.

Jonathan So

Regional Director

The Taft School

Hong Kong

Jonathan is responsible for expanding SubstitOud's operations in Hong Kong. He is also the founder of TorsionTech, providing affordable prosthetics for pets.

Rex Tsang

Regional Director

The Taft School

China

Rex leads SubstitOud's expansion into China, Nepal, and Bhutan, where incense is burned daily in homes, monasteries, and roadside shrines. He builds the distributor and temple relationships that carry low-smoke incense into those communities.
