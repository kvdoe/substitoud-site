Privacy

Last updated November 22, 2026

This page explains what SubstitOud Co., Ltd. collects when you use substitoud.com, and what we do with it.

What we collect

When you place an order

Your name, email address, phone number, shipping address and what you bought. You enter these on a payment page run by Stripe, our payment processor, and Stripe shares them with us so we can ship your order.

Payment details

Your card details go to Stripe only. We never see or store your full card number.

When you email us or send a wholesale inquiry

Whatever you include in the message, such as your name, organization, phone number and email address.

Your cart

The number of boxes in your cart is saved in your own browser so it is still there when you come back. It is not sent to us until you check out.

What we do not do

  • We do not use advertising trackers or analytics cookies on this site.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you ask for it.

How we use your information

  • To take payment, ship your order and send your receipt.
  • To reach you by email or phone if there is a problem with your order.
  • To answer your questions and handle returns.
  • To keep the order and tax records the law requires.

Who else handles it

  • Stripe processes payments and holds order records. See Stripe's privacy policy.
  • The shipping carrier receives your name and address to deliver your order.
  • Cloudflare hosts this website and, like any web host, processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and block abuse.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site, which means your browser requests them from Google.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as tax and accounting rules require, and emails for as long as needed to help you.

Your choices

Email contact@substitoud.com to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, or to ask us to delete it. We will delete what we are not required to keep.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new date.
