Shipping and returns

Last updated November 22, 2026

Shipping

Where we ship

Online orders ship to addresses in the United States. For anywhere else, email contact@substitoud.com.

Cost

Shipping is $3.99 per order. Orders of 3 or more boxes ship free.

Timing

We pack orders within 3 business days. Most arrive 3 to 7 business days after they ship. These are estimates, and carriers can run late around holidays.

Wrong address

Check your address on the payment page before you pay. If you spot a mistake afterward, email us right away with your order number. Once a package has shipped we cannot redirect it.

Lost or late packages

If your order has not arrived 12 business days after it shipped, email us. We will trace it with the carrier and replace or refund it if it is lost.

Returns

Unopened boxes

You can return unopened boxes within 30 days of delivery for a refund of what you paid for them. Email us first with your order number so we can send return instructions. Return postage is at your cost.

Opened boxes

Incense is consumable, so we cannot take back opened boxes unless something was wrong with them.

Damaged or incorrect orders

If a box arrives damaged, or we sent the wrong quantity, email contact@substitoud.com within 14 days of delivery with your order number and a photo. We will replace it or refund it, and you will not pay return postage.

Refunds

Refunds go back to the original payment method. After we approve a refund, banks usually take 5 to 10 business days to show it.

Bundle orders

If you return part of a bundle order, the refund is based on the price per box that the boxes you keep would have qualified for.

Contact

SubstitOud Co., Ltd. Email contact@substitoud.com.
