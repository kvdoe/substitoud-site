How we started.

The Anh's Story.

At 13, my grandfather passed away. Following Vietnamese tradition, I burned incense daily for 49 days. By the end, I'd developed a chronic cough.

This was unacceptable for me: a cultural tradition should not come with side effects.

So, I spent the next 2 years to develop a solution. Meeting with leaders of the industry for advice, and collaborating with Karan on the formulation.

After developing the formula, I partnered with farmers to grow sustainable oud for my incense.

In 2025, I received a utility patent and launched pilot sales in select pagodas across Vietnam.

Now, in 2026, we're expanding our mission to
Hong Kong, China, Nepal,
Bhutan, Dubai, and the USA.

Incense that preserves tradition.
