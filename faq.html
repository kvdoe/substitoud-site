Questions and answers

The incense

How much smoke does it make?

93% less than a regular stick. That keeps the air in your home far cleaner, which matters when incense burns indoors day after day. The smoke test video shows the two side by side.

What is in it?

Coconut-fiber charcoal powder, agarwood powder, vetiver powder, joss powder, pomelo peel and water.

What is agarwood, or oud?

Agarwood is the fragrant resinous wood of the Aquilaria tree, known as oud. Wild trees have been heavily depleted, so we use sustainably cultivated agarwood.

Why is there no bamboo core?

The sticks are pressed without a core through our own in-house method, which is part of our utility patent. It also means they are more fragile than cored sticks, so handle them gently.

How many sticks are in a box?

60 sticks.

Is a holder included?

No. The box contains incense sticks only.

How do I burn it safely?

Light the tip, let the flame catch, then blow it out so the tip glows. Burn within sight and never leave burning incense unattended. Keep it away from flammable objects, drafts, children and pets.
