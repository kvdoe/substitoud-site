What is in the box

60 incense sticks, 50 g net, in a dark inner box with the crane and turtle sleeve.

The sticks are pressed without a bamboo core, so handle them gently. The holders in the photos are not included.

Ingredients

Coconut-fiber charcoal powder, agarwood powder, vetiver powder, joss powder, pomelo peel and water. That is the full list printed on the sleeve.

How to burn it safely

Stand a stick in a holder on a heat-resistant, fireproof surface. Light the tip, let the flame catch, then blow it out so the tip glows.

  • Burn within sight and never leave burning incense unattended.
  • Keep away from flammable objects, drafts, children and pets.

Before you buy

Is it completely smokeless?

Virtually. A stick still glows and gives off its scent, with far less visible smoke than a regular stick. The side-by-side video on our home page shows the difference.

One or two boxes are $4.99 each. From 3 boxes every box is $4.49, from 6 every box is $3.99, and from 12 every box is $3.49. The discount shows in your cart before you pay.

The online store ships to US addresses, free of charge. Outside the US, SubstitOud is sold through shops and temples in Vietnam, Nepal, Hong Kong, Bhutan and China. Email contact@substitoud.com and we will point you to the nearest one.
